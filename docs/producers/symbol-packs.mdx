## Custom pack

Define your own universe in `config/user.yaml`:

```yaml
universe:
  symbols: ["BTC", "ETH", "SOL"]
```

<Tip>
Start smaller than you think. A tight universe improves signal quality and reduces producer noise.
</Tip>
